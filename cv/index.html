// experience

Dec 2022 – Present Deltares

Geotechnical Advisor & Digital Engineer

Researcher and advisor in the Safe and Resilient Infrastructure department, working across railway embankment stability, geotechnical data interpretation, and applied machine learning. Core contributor to RESET, a multi-year research programme with ProRail and TU Delft, developing improved soil strength frameworks and running large-scale PLAXIS parametric studies through Python API automation. Developed and published SchemaGAN, a conditional GAN for automated subsurface schematisation from CPT data (Computers and Geotechnics, 2025), and co-developed GeoSyn for synthetic geotechnical data generation. Builds Python data pipelines and interactive analysis tools for project teams and clients, and develops monitoring workflows for fibre-optic sensing systems on railway infrastructure.

2015 – 2021 University of Costa Rica

Geotechnical Engineer

Led probabilistic slope stability and landslide hazard studies for municipalities, infrastructure clients, and communities across Costa Rica, combining field investigation, laboratory testing, GIS-based hazard mapping, and limit-equilibrium analysis. Provided post-event field assessment to the National Emergency Commission following Hurricane Otto (2016), supporting evacuation and return-to-site decisions in affected mountain areas. Applied bearing capacity, settlement, and back-analysis methods to site-specific instability problems across urban and road infrastructure settings.

2019 – 2020 Inter-American Development Bank

Geotechnical Risk Consultant

Provided geotechnical risk advisory for infrastructure and community instability problems across South America, working in multidisciplinary teams with engineers, policy-makers, and risk specialists. Projects spanned post-landslide risk assessment at the Quito Metro construction site in Ecuador, a multi-phase technical engagement on the Kantutani urban landslide for the La Paz Municipality in Bolivia, and a full landslide catalogue and risk assessment for an active mountain road corridor in Salta Province, Argentina.

2017 – 2019 University of Costa Rica

Lecturer and Thesis Supervisor

Lectured in geology fundamentals and introduction to geotechnics at undergraduate level, covering laboratory practice, in-situ testing, and field applications. Served on thesis committees for licentiate dissertations on landslide probability, slope stability, and hydrogeology.

2018 – 2019 Geostru Software

Product Development Consultant

Provided technical feedback on geotechnical analysis features during software development cycles and contributed to the internal training programme for new users.
