// experience

Dec 2022 – Present Deltares

Geotechnical Researcher and Consultant

  • Core contributor to RESET, a multi-year research programme with ProRail and TU Delft, combining CPT, borehole, laboratory and centrifuge evidence with PLAXIS 2D/3D, limit equilibrium analysis and Python automation to improve embankment stability assessment.
  • Integrated 34 CPTs with geological data and stochastic methods to assess critical train speed along a 15 km railway corridor.
  • Built Python workflows to detect train passages and validate fibre optic monitoring against accelerometer measurements.
  • Developed and published SchemaGAN, a generative AI method for subsurface schematisation from CPT data (Computers and Geotechnics, 2025), with related work on GeoSyn and Deep Reinforcement Learning for site investigation planning.
  • Builds reproducible Python and Jupyter workflows, automates PLAXIS through its API, and contributes to DGEOLIB Plus.
2014 – 2021 University of Costa Rica

Geotechnical Engineer, Researcher and Lecturer

  • Planned and carried out site investigations (CPT, SPT, boreholes, geological mapping, MASW, electrical resistivity tomography) to build ground models for foundations, slopes and infrastructure.
  • Performed and interpreted laboratory testing (direct shear, triaxial, oedometer, Atterberg limits) across volcanic, residual and alluvial soils.
  • Led probabilistic landslide studies using the PISA method at municipal, community and site scales, and supported the National Emergency Commission following Hurricane Otto in 2016.
  • Carried out limit equilibrium analyses for slopes, landfills and infrastructure sites, and lectured in soil mechanics and geology while supervising student research.
2018 – 2020 Inter-American Development Bank

Geotechnical Risk Consultant

  • Supported remediation planning for the Kantutani landslide in La Paz, Bolivia, helping define terms of reference for follow up investigations with the municipality.
  • Assessed spoil disposal suitability at the Quito Metro project in Ecuador following a fatal slope failure, and reviewed safer alternatives.
  • Carried out landslide mapping and a corridor risk assessment along Route RP33 in Salta, Argentina, recommending monitoring and short term risk reduction measures.
2018 – 2019 Geostru Software

Technical Support Specialist

  • Produced technical training material and tested new features for geotechnical software users across Latin America.
  • Gave structured feedback to the development team to support user adoption.
