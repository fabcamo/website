Fabián A. Campos Montero

// Geotechnical Engineer & Researcher | Infrastructure, Soil Mechanics & AI

Geotechnical engineer and researcher at Deltares with over 10 years of experience across geotechnical engineering, site investigation, monitoring, natural hazards and applied research. His work combines a strong geotechnical foundation with AI and data methods, from CPT based subsurface schematisation and machine learning to field investigation, laboratory testing and fibre optic monitoring, alongside slope and embankment stability and probabilistic landslide assessment. Across research and consultancy projects, he turns complex geotechnical data into clearer engineering decisions.

// experience

Dec 2022 – Present Deltares Delft, The Netherlands

Geotechnical Researcher and Consultant

  • Helped strengthen the technical basis for assessing Dutch railway embankments in ProRail's RESET programme, combining site investigation, laboratory and centrifuge data with PLAXIS 2D/3D, limit equilibrium analyses and Python automation to support decisions on running more, faster and heavier trains safely.
  • Developed machine learning methods relating dark fibre measurements along railway tracks to free field accelerometer data, giving a scalable way to screen larger parts of the network for locations with higher vibration risk.
  • Advanced automated subsurface interpretation with SchemaGAN, a generative AI method that creates geotechnical cross sections from limited CPT data, with related work on GeoSyn for synthetic ground data and Deep Reinforcement Learning for site investigation planning.
  • Made site characterisation and numerical studies faster and more reproducible by building Python workflows around CPT data, statistical parameterisation and PLAXIS, and by further developing the DGEOLIB Plus software.
  • Turns complex geotechnical and data analyses into clear engineering results for clients, researchers and multidisciplinary teams.
2014 – 2021 University of Costa Rica Geomechanics Laboratory, San José, Costa Rica

Geotechnical Engineer, Researcher and Lecturer

  • Carried out probabilistic landslide assessment using PISA methods, combining field, laboratory and GIS data under dry, saturated and seismic conditions.
  • Planned and interpreted site investigations, including CPT, SPT, boreholes, sampling, geological mapping, MASW, seismic refraction and electrical resistivity tomography, to build ground models for natural slopes, landfills, mines and quarries.
  • Carried out limit equilibrium analyses and foundation design, including bearing capacity and settlement, based on interpreted geotechnical parameters.
  • Performed and interpreted laboratory testing, including direct shear, triaxial and oedometer tests, to characterise soil strength and compressibility.
  • Led field investigations and emergency assessments, including the 2016 response to Hurricane Otto.
  • Taught soil mechanics and geology courses and supervised student research and junior staff in the Geomechanics Laboratory.
2018 – 2020 Inter-American Development Bank South America

Geotechnical Risk Consultant

  • Kantutani, La Paz — assessed the mechanics of a municipal solid waste mass at the Kantutani landslide, defining terms of reference for follow up investigation and coordinating with the municipality on geotechnical risk recommendations.
  • Quito Metro — reviewed spoil management following a fatal slope failure, defining terms of reference for further geotechnical investigation and assessing the affected disposal site and safer alternatives.
  • RP33 — landslide mapping — carried out field mapping and built a GIS corridor inventory of instability mechanisms along Route RP33 in Salta, Argentina, identifying priority risk areas.
  • RP33 — risk management — defined terms of reference for further studies and recommended slope monitoring, early warning and short term risk reduction measures.
Aug – Nov 2022 Royal HaskoningDHV The Netherlands

Geotechnical Engineer Intern

  • Compared conventional and load distributed anchor systems for the Oosterweel Link project using analytical calculations and PLAXIS, helping assess how anchor length affects performance and the use of double grouted anchors.
2018 – 2019 Geostru Software Part time

Technical Support Specialist

  • Produced technical training material, tested new features and gave structured feedback to the development team, supporting software adoption for geotechnical users across Latin America.
2013 Diquís Hydroelectric Project, ICE Costa Rica

Intern Geologist

  • Carried out geological and geomorphological mapping and in situ permeability testing, interpreting field data and developing a GIS database for the project.
